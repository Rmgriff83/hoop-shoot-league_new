Privacy Policy

Hoop Shoot League · Last updated November 6, 2026

Hoop Shoot League ("the game") is made by Ross Griffus ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, who else handles it, and how to delete it. If you have a question, email CONTACT_EMAIL.

The short version

  • You never need an email, a password or your real name. The game creates an anonymous player account when you choose a player name.
  • Your player name, tag and hometown are shown to other players on boards and in online matches.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. On iPhone, Google only uses your device's advertising identifier if you allow tracking when asked.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time in the game: Settings > PLAYER > DELETE ACCOUNT.
  • I do not sell your data.

What the game stores on my server

The game's server runs on Cloudflare. It keeps:

WhatWhy
A random player ID and a secret key the game creates on your phoneTo recognise your account without a password.
Your player name, its two-digit tag and your levelTo show you on boards, in online matches and to other players.
Your hometown, if you set oneShown to your opponent in online matches. Names and hometowns pass through a word filter.
Scores, time-trial bests, online match results and your ranked ratingFor the boards, matchmaking and the ranked ladder.
A copy of your saved progress (settings, items, league progress)So your progress can follow you to a new phone.
Your Game Center player ID and display name, if you are signed in to Game CenterTo link your account to Game Center and restore it on a new phone.
Purchase records (product, transaction ID, date, refunds)To give you what you bought, keep Remove Ads working and handle refunds.

The server also sees your IP address with each request. It is used only to limit abuse (for example, how many accounts one network can create in an hour). It is not stored with your account, and the counters are deleted within a few days.

What stays on your phone

Your game progress and settings are saved on your device. Deleting the app removes them from the phone.

The game has no chat, no analytics service of its own and does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

Ads, tracking and your choices

  • Tracking: on iPhone you can allow or refuse tracking when the game asks. You can change this later in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Remove Ads: the one-time Remove Ads purchase turns ads off. Once you own it, the ads SDK is not started at all.
  • Personalised ads: you can limit them in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising, and through Google's ad settings.

Deleting your data

In the game, go to Settings > PLAYER > DELETE ACCOUNT and tap it twice. This deletes your account from the server straight away, together with your name, hometown, saved progress, scores and Game Center link. Results of online matches you played are kept as the other player's record of the game. They do not show your name. Purchase records needed for refunds and tax may be kept by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies.

If you can't open the game, email CONTACT_EMAIL with your player name and tag, and I will delete the account for you.

How long data is kept

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Abuse-limit counters are deleted within a few days.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email me and I will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, and to object to how it is used. Most of this you can do in the game. For anything else, email CONTACT_EMAIL.
