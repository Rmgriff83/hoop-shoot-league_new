Common questions

How do I shoot?

Swipe up on the ball. How you swipe decides the shot: a soft, smooth flick drops in, a hard one hits the back iron, a flat one rattles out.

I bought something and didn't get it.

Leave the Ticket Store open for a moment and check your connection. Tickets are credited once the purchase is confirmed. For Remove Ads, use RESTORE PURCHASES in the Ticket Store or in Settings > MORE. If it still hasn't arrived, email me with the date of the purchase.

How do I get Remove Ads back on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the Ticket Store or Settings > MORE and tap RESTORE PURCHASES.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Sign in to Game Center on both phones. Your account is linked to Game Center and comes back when you install the game on the new phone.

Online matchmaking is taking a long time.

Online ranked matches pair you with another real player. At quiet times it can take a while. The league and time trials are always available.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?

In the game: Settings > PLAYER > DELETE ACCOUNT, tap twice. This deletes your online account and its data. See the Privacy Policy for what that removes.

How do I stop ads tracking me?

Choose "Ask App Not to Track" when asked, or change it later in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. The one-time Remove Ads purchase turns ads off completely.
